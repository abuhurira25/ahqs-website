FAQ

Toolkit questions

Is this an official CAP, ISO, AABB or JCI product?

No. AHQS is an independent consulting business. The toolkit is an independent readiness resource and is not issued, endorsed or certified by an accrediting organization.

Can I use the toolkit for ISO 15189 or CAP preparation?

It is designed around general laboratory quality and inspection-readiness activities such as gap assessment, competency, evidence, QC/PT, audits and CAPA. Always use the official requirements applicable to your organization.

Can AHQS review our readiness after we use the toolkit?

Yes. You can book a focused laboratory quality consultation or request a broader gap assessment or mock inspection.
