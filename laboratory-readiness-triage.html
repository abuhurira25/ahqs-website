COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you reserve your call

Is the 20-minute readiness triage really complimentary?

Yes. The introductory triage is complimentary and carries no obligation to purchase consulting services.

What should I bring?

Bring one priority concern and, if relevant, your assessment timeline. Do not send PHI, patient-identifiable information, confidential accreditation materials or proprietary records.

Can AHQS support laboratories outside the United States?

Yes. The triage and many readiness services can be delivered virtually worldwide. On-site support may be arranged by agreement.

Does AHQS guarantee accreditation?

No. AHQS provides independent readiness and quality-improvement support. Accreditation decisions remain with the applicable accrediting organization.
