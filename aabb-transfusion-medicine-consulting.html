Frequently asked questions

Does AHQS provide AABB accreditation readiness support?

Yes. AHQS provides independent consulting designed to help organizations evaluate readiness, identify gaps, strengthen quality systems and prepare for accreditation activities.

Can AHQS support hospital blood banks?

Yes. Support can include transfusion-service quality systems, inspection readiness, audits, documentation review, risk management and quality improvement.

Do you provide international consulting?

Yes. AHQS is designed to support healthcare and laboratory organizations internationally through remote and project-based consulting arrangements.

Is AHQS affiliated with AABB?

No. AHQS is an independent healthcare quality consulting organization and does not represent or imply endorsement by AABB or any accreditation organization.
