Practical ISO 15189 support for clinical laboratories

Maintaining ISO 15189 accreditation requires more than preparing for an assessment. Laboratories must continuously demonstrate competence, effective quality management, controlled processes, reliable evidence and sustained corrective action.

AHQS provides flexible external support to laboratories that need additional accreditation expertise without adding permanent full-time resources.

ISO 15189 consulting services

ISO 15189 Gap Assessment

Structured review of your current laboratory quality system against ISO 15189 requirements to identify compliance gaps, evidence weaknesses and priority risks.

Mock Accreditation Assessment

Inspection-style readiness assessment designed to identify findings before your accreditation body does.

Quality Management System Review

Review of laboratory governance, document control, internal audits, quality indicators, competence, risk management, CAPA and management review processes.

Corrective Action Planning

Support with root-cause analysis, corrective action development, implementation evidence and effectiveness monitoring.

Internal Audit Support

Development or strengthening of laboratory internal audit programs and focused audits in areas of accreditation risk.

Ongoing Accreditation Readiness

Periodic external support to help laboratories remain continuously ready rather than preparing only when an assessment is approaching.

Areas we can review

  • Quality management system effectiveness
  • Personnel competence and authorization
  • Equipment, calibration and maintenance controls
  • Pre-examination, examination and post-examination processes
  • Document and record control
  • Internal audits and management review
  • Nonconformities and corrective action
  • Risk management and improvement
  • Quality indicators and performance monitoring
  • Sample receipt, handling, storage and traceability
  • Laboratory information and result reporting controls

ISO 15189 consulting FAQs

What is ISO 15189 consulting?

ISO 15189 consulting supports laboratories in evaluating accreditation gaps, strengthening their quality management system and preparing evidence for assessment.

Can AHQS help an already accredited laboratory?

Yes. Support can focus on surveillance readiness, reaccreditation, internal audits, corrective actions, quality indicators and continuous compliance.

Can AHQS perform a mock ISO 15189 assessment?

Yes. A mock assessment can simulate the accreditation process and identify high-priority gaps before an external assessment.

Need additional ISO 15189 accreditation support?

Start with a focused discussion about your laboratory's current accreditation status, upcoming assessment and highest-risk quality gaps.
