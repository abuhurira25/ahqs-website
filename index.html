FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers before you get started

Common questions about AHQS, accreditation readiness and consulting.

Does AHQS provide accreditation?

AHQS provides independent accreditation-readiness and quality consulting. Accreditation decisions remain with the relevant accrediting body.

Do you support both hospitals and laboratories?

Yes. AHQS supports hospitals, healthcare organizations, clinical laboratories and transfusion services across accreditation, clinical audit, patient safety, risk, laboratory quality and excellence.

Can support be provided virtually?

Yes. Many reviews, coaching sessions and readiness activities can be delivered remotely, with on-site work available by agreement.
