CAP

Inspection readiness anchored in evidence and daily practice

Experience strengthening laboratory processes, competency evidence, QC/PT oversight, document control, findings management, mock inspection and leadership visibility for CAP accreditation pathways.

Outcome focus: ensure that documented requirements, staff practice and retrievable evidence align before external inspection.

Outcome focus: help healthcare teams demonstrate safe, governed and consistently implemented systems—not simply complete documents.
