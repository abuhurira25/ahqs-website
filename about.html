OUR PURPOSE

We help healthcare organizations make accreditation readiness, clinical governance and excellence visible, organized and sustainable.

Accreditation and quality challenges rarely come from a complete lack of effort. More often, hospitals and laboratories are dealing with fragmented systems, competing priorities, inconsistent implementation, recurring findings, weak audit follow-up and uncertainty about whether the evidence truly demonstrates reliable practice.

AHQS provides an independent, structured perspective across accreditation readiness, clinical audit, laboratory quality, patient safety, risk management and organizational excellence. We help teams identify where risk exists, where evidence is weak, where accountability is unclear and where stronger governance can reduce survey pressure and improve day-to-day performance.

“The objective is not to look ready on survey day. It is to build a healthcare organization that can demonstrate quality, safety, governance and excellence every day.”

FOUNDER & LEAD EXPERT

Senior healthcare quality, accreditation and laboratory leader with multidisciplinary experience

AHQS is founded by a healthcare quality and laboratory professional with more than two decades of experience across medical laboratory science, transfusion medicine, quality management, patient safety, risk management, accreditation and organizational excellence.

His background includes leadership of ISO 15189 implementation and recertification across multiple medical laboratories, hospital and primary-care accreditation programs, CAP-related laboratory quality activities, transfusion-medicine operations, clinical-risk programs, internal audits, root-cause analysis, quality indicators, document control, competency systems and enterprise performance improvement.

He has held senior leadership responsibilities across multi-hospital healthcare systems and brings together technical laboratory expertise with healthcare quality, governance and business-management experience. His credentials include medical laboratory certification, an MBA in Quality Management, CPHQ, ISO Lead Auditor, EFQM Lead Assessor and CAP inspection experience.

The value to clients is a practical combination of accreditation knowledge, clinical operations insight, audit discipline, risk-based thinking and implementation experience—focused on helping organizations convert standards into sustainable day-to-day practice.
