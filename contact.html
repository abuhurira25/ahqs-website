FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Before you contact us

Does AHQS provide accreditation or certification?

No. AHQS provides independent quality-improvement and accreditation-readiness consulting. Accreditation and certification decisions remain with the applicable authorized organizations.

Can AHQS support laboratories virtually?

Yes. Many coaching, document review, readiness, CAPA and quality-management activities can be performed virtually. On-site services can be discussed depending on scope and location.

Do we have to purchase the toolkit before consulting?

No. The digital toolkit and consulting services are separate. The best starting point depends on your laboratory’s needs.

What happens during the free 20-minute discovery call?

We focus on your main quality or readiness challenge, clarify what is happening and identify the most appropriate next step. It is not a generic sales presentation.

Can we send patient records for review?

Please do not send PHI or unnecessary patient-identifiable information through the website or ordinary email. Any future engagement involving sensitive information should use an appropriate secure process.
