EXPERIENCE & OUTCOMES

Accreditation experience shaped by implementation, governance and real-world readiness

AHQS draws on more than two decades of international healthcare and laboratory quality experience across government and private organizations, including professional expertise strengthened in the United States. The delivery history behind AHQS spans ground-up quality-system development, clinical governance, risk, audit, patient safety and preparation through ISO 15189, CAP and JCI accreditation journeys.

FROM FOUNDATION TO EXTERNAL ASSESSMENT

Evidence of experience is strongest when it follows the entire journey

The work represented here is not limited to pre-survey document checking. It includes designing and strengthening the systems organizations need to operate, demonstrate and sustain quality before, during and after accreditation.

Ground-Up SystemsQuality-system architecture, governance, document control, competency, risk, audit and performance processes.
Readiness & Mock ReviewGap assessment, evidence sampling, tracers, mock assessment, priority action planning and staff preparation.
