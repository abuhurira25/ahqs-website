Healthcare Quality & Laboratory Accreditation Consulting Services

AHQS supports hospitals, clinical laboratories and healthcare organizations with accreditation, reaccreditation, clinical audit, quality-system improvement and excellence programs designed to strengthen readiness, evidence, governance and sustainable performance.

Laboratory Inspection Readiness Assessment

Structured review of readiness, evidence and quality risks before inspection day. We help identify high-priority gaps, clarify what needs attention and establish a practical action plan.

Mock Inspection & Gap Assessment

Inspection-style review of selected quality systems, records, staff readiness, competency, QC/PT, CAPA and evidence retrieval. Designed to reveal vulnerabilities before the real inspection.

Quality Management System Review

Evaluate how effectively your QMS connects document control, indicators, audits, nonconformances, risk, CAPA, competency and management oversight.

🔄

CAPA & Root Cause Analysis Support

Strengthen problem definition, contributing-factor analysis, corrective-action design, accountability and effectiveness review—especially for recurring findings.

👥

Personnel Competency & Training Review

Identify missing, overdue, incomplete or inconsistent competency and training documentation and improve visibility of future due dates and follow-up actions.

🩸

Transfusion Medicine & Blood Bank Quality Support

Specialized support for Blood Bank quality systems, competency, documentation, risk assessment, internal audits, traceability and inspection readiness.

🎯

Quality & Accreditation Coaching

One-to-one virtual support for laboratory leaders working through inspection preparation, accreditation-readiness, quality-system or documentation challenges.

Laboratory Digital Quality Tools

Editable dashboards, checklists, trackers and templates that help laboratories organize readiness, CAPA, evidence, competency and quality management.

🤝

Ongoing Quality Partner Support

Recurring support for laboratories that need regular quality follow-up, leadership visibility, accountability and ongoing inspection-readiness monitoring.

🏥

JCI Accreditation & Reaccreditation Readiness Support

Comprehensive preparation support for hospitals pursuing Joint Commission International accreditation or reaccreditation. Services may include gap analysis, chapter-by-chapter readiness review, policy and evidence alignment, tracer preparation, mock survey activities, leadership readiness, corrective-action planning and sustained compliance support.

🧬

ISO 15189 Accreditation & Reaccreditation Readiness Support

End-to-end support for medical laboratories preparing for ISO 15189 accreditation or reaccreditation, including QMS development, gap assessment, internal audit readiness, risk management, document control, competency, quality indicators, corrective actions, management review and assessment preparation.

🧪

CAP Accreditation & Reaccreditation Readiness

Independent laboratory accreditation-readiness support focused on strengthening quality systems, documentation, personnel competency, QC/PT oversight, internal audits, evidence retrieval, corrective-action effectiveness and inspection preparation. AHQS does not act on behalf of CAP and does not provide official CAP inspection services.

🩸

AABB Accreditation & Reaccreditation Readiness Support

Quality and readiness support for transfusion services, blood banks and related organizations preparing for AABB accreditation or reaccreditation. Focus areas may include quality systems, policies, competency, traceability, process control, records, internal audits, corrective actions, risk management and survey-readiness planning.

🩺

Clinical Audit & Governance Review

Structured clinical and healthcare audits designed to evaluate compliance, process reliability, governance, patient-safety controls and improvement opportunities. AHQS supports audit planning, criteria development, evidence review, findings, action plans, follow-up and closure monitoring.

🏆

Healthcare Excellence & Performance Improvement

Advisory support for healthcare organizations seeking stronger organizational performance, quality culture and excellence. Services can include excellence-framework alignment, KPI architecture, leadership review, improvement portfolios, governance systems, benchmarking, risk integration and preparation for quality or excellence assessments.

🛡️

Patient Safety & Healthcare Risk Management

Independent support for patient safety systems, healthcare risk management, incident investigation, root cause analysis, CAPA and clinical governance.

🧪

Laboratory Quality Management System Consulting

Support for laboratory QMS development, internal audits, document control, competency, quality indicators, CAPA, risk management and accreditation readiness.
