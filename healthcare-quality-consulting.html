Our Approach

From accreditation gaps to sustainable improvement

1. Discover

Understand the organization, accreditation objective, quality risks and current challenges.

2. Assess

Evaluate systems, documents, evidence, processes and implementation against relevant requirements.

3. Prioritize

Identify high-risk gaps and establish a practical improvement roadmap.

4. Implement

Support policies, quality systems, corrective actions, training and process improvement.

5. Verify

Review implementation effectiveness and evidence of sustained compliance.

6. Sustain

Build systems that remain effective beyond the accreditation survey.

Frequently asked questions

Does AHQS work internationally?

Yes. AHQS is positioned to support hospitals, laboratories and healthcare organizations internationally through remote consulting and agreed project-based support.

Can AHQS help a laboratory prepare for ISO 15189 accreditation?

Yes. Our ISO 15189 services include gap assessment, QMS review, internal audit support, competency systems, risk management, CAPA and assessment readiness.

Can you help hospitals prepare for accreditation?

Yes. AHQS can assess organizational readiness, identify gaps, review evidence, support corrective actions and conduct structured readiness activities.

Do you provide patient safety and risk-management consulting?

Yes. Services can include incident-management review, root cause analysis, CAPA, risk assessment, clinical governance and patient-safety improvement.

How does an organization start?

Start with an initial consultation so that AHQS can understand the organization, accreditation objective, current challenges and desired outcomes.
