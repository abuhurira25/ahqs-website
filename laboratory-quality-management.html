Build a Laboratory QMS That Works Every Day

A laboratory quality management system should do more than produce documents for an inspection. It should create reliable processes, clear accountability, measurable performance, competent personnel, effective risk controls and continuous improvement throughout the total testing process.

AHQS provides independent laboratory quality consulting designed around the operational realities of clinical laboratories and the requirements commonly encountered during laboratory accreditation and regulatory assessments.

Laboratory Quality Management Services

QMS Development

Design and improvement of laboratory quality systems, governance, responsibilities, processes and quality-management structures.

Gap Assessment

Structured review of laboratory practices against applicable quality, accreditation and organizational requirements.

Document Control

Improvement of policies, procedures, forms, records, document approval, revision control and document lifecycle management.

Internal Audits

Risk-based laboratory internal audit programs designed to identify gaps before external inspection or accreditation assessment.

CAPA Management

Root-cause-based corrective and preventive action systems with responsibility, deadlines, evidence and effectiveness monitoring.

Laboratory Risk Management

Identification and control of risks throughout pre-analytical, analytical and post-analytical laboratory processes.

Personnel Competency

Support for training, competency assessment, authorization, continuing education and personnel documentation systems.

Quality Indicators

Selection, monitoring and interpretation of meaningful laboratory quality indicators, trends and performance measures.

Management Review

Development of structured management-review processes that turn quality information into leadership decisions and improvement actions.

Nonconformity Management

Systems for identifying, documenting, investigating and learning from laboratory nonconformities and recurring problems.

Proficiency Testing & EQA

Review of proficiency-testing and external-quality-assessment processes, investigation and corrective-action practices.

Inspection Readiness

Preparation of staff, documentation, evidence and laboratory processes for external accreditation and regulatory assessment.

The Laboratory Quality Improvement Cycle

01 — Assess Understand current laboratory processes, risks, gaps and performance.
02 — Design Develop practical systems, responsibilities, controls and documentation.
03 — Implement Embed quality requirements into routine laboratory operations.
04 — Improve Audit, measure, investigate, correct and continuously strengthen performance.

Quality Across the Total Testing Process

Pre-Analytical Quality

Patient identification, specimen collection, labeling, transportation, receipt, acceptance and storage.

Analytical Quality

Method performance, quality control, equipment, reagents, calibration, verification and testing processes.

Post-Analytical Quality

Result review, reporting, critical values, turnaround time, records, communication and clinical service performance.
