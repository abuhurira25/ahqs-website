Privacy & Accessibility

How AHQS handles information submitted through this website and connected services.

Information AHQS May Receive

If you contact AHQS by email, phone, calendar booking, or the website inquiry function, AHQS may receive the contact and business information you choose to provide, such as your name, organization, email, phone number, service of interest and inquiry details.

No PHI Through This Website

Do not submit protected health information (PHI), patient-identifiable data, medical records, passwords, confidential accreditation materials or other sensitive records through the public website, the inquiry form or ordinary email. If a future consulting engagement requires sensitive information, appropriate confidentiality, security and contractual safeguards will be evaluated before any transfer occurs.

How Information Is Used

Information you provide may be used to respond to inquiries, schedule consultations, prepare proposals, deliver requested services, provide customer support and maintain legitimate business records. AHQS does not sell personal information obtained through this website.

Third-Party Services

Appointment scheduling is provided through Google Calendar and digital-product checkout/delivery is provided through Payhip. Those services may process information under their own privacy and security policies. AHQS does not control their independent data practices.

Cookies & Technical Data

AHQS uses Google Analytics 4 to understand general website usage, such as page views, device type, traffic source and engagement. AHQS does not use the website to collect protected health information (PHI), and does not sell personal information. Google and other linked third-party services may process technical information or use cookies according to their own privacy policies.

Retention & Security

AHQS uses reasonable administrative practices to protect business contact information. No Internet or email system can be guaranteed completely secure. Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for legitimate business, contractual or legal purposes.

Your Choices

You may ask AHQS to correct or delete business contact information previously provided, subject to legitimate recordkeeping or legal obligations. Contact AHQS at info@ahqshealthcare.com.

Effective date: August 12, 2026. This website policy is provided for general business transparency and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel as AHQS grows, enters new jurisdictions, or changes how data is collected or services are delivered.

Accessibility Commitment

Our Commitment

AHQS aims to make its website reasonably usable by people with disabilities and to improve accessibility as the website evolves.

Design Practices

The website uses responsive layouts, semantic headings, readable contrast, descriptive link text and scalable text. Key actions such as consultation booking and contact options are available as text links.

Known Limitations

Third-party services such as Google Calendar and Payhip are controlled by their respective providers. Their accessibility may differ from the AHQS website.
