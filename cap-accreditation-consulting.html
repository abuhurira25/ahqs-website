CAP Readiness Support

Practical preparation for CAP laboratory accreditation

CAP accreditation requires more than having policies in place. Laboratories must demonstrate consistent implementation, staff competency, quality control, proficiency testing performance, document control, corrective action and evidence of continuous quality oversight.

📋

AHQS Approach

Inspection readiness built around evidence

Our approach focuses on what inspectors are likely to evaluate: whether the requirement is understood, implemented, documented, monitored and sustained.

Rather than simply identifying checklist gaps, AHQS helps laboratories prioritize findings by risk and build practical actions that can strengthen day-to-day operations.

Frequently Asked Questions

CAP accreditation consulting FAQs

Can AHQS help us prepare for a CAP inspection?

Yes. AHQS can perform structured readiness assessments, checklist-based gap reviews, evidence review and mock inspection preparation.

Can AHQS review CAP checklist compliance?

Yes. The review can focus on selected laboratory sections or broader laboratory accreditation requirements depending on project scope.

Can you help with deficiencies after an inspection?

Yes. AHQS can help analyze deficiencies, identify root causes and develop practical corrective action plans with evidence of implementation.

Do you support competency and QC readiness?

Yes. Competency, quality control, proficiency testing, document control and other quality-system elements may be included in the readiness review.

Does AHQS support laboratories internationally?

Yes. AHQS is positioned to support laboratories internationally through remote consulting and agreed project-based engagements.
